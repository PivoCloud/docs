## Where your data lives

Your databases, apps and backups stay in Algeria. That is the default, not an option you switch on.

## Held in Algeria

This applies to every account.

- **Your databases**: Each database and its contents are hosted in one of our two datacenters in Algeria.
- **Your apps**: We build and run them in Algeria: the built image, the running app, its settings, its environment variables and its logs.
- **Database backups**: They are kept in our two datacenters in Algeria.

## Outside Algeria

These services are outside Algeria or are run by a company outside Algeria. Each is named by kind, with what it receives.

- **The provider that sends our emails**: Your email address and the messages we send you, such as verification, billing notices and receipts.
- **The provider that measures website use**: Page views on our website. If you are signed in and accepted analytics, your account ID and email address. Never your app data or database contents.
- **The mailbox that receives the emails you send us**: Anything you write to us by email.
- **WhatsApp, if you write to us there**: The conversation.
- **Our DNS provider**: The addresses of your apps and databases, including the name you chose. It only answers address lookups. Your traffic does not pass through it. It is run by a company outside Algeria and may answer from many countries, Algeria included.
- **Where you keep your source code, for example GitHub**: Your code stays there. To deploy, we fetch a copy and build it in Algeria. We also ask it whether your repository can be reached.
- **The host of our status page**: Health signals about our platform: counts and internal identifiers, never the address of your database. In a rare alarm, the names of up to three of your apps, which also reach our team’s alert chat. Your browser also loads the status badge from it when you visit our website.
- **The host of our documentation**: Nothing of yours. Only your visit, when you read the documentation. It is run by a company outside Algeria and may answer from many countries, Algeria included.
- **An AI agent you choose to connect**: Whatever you let the agent read.

Card payment: you type your card details on our payment partner’s page, never on ours.

## The law

The Official Journal publishes these laws in Arabic and in French. The Arabic text is the original and the French text is a translation, both published in the Official Journal. This page states facts. It is not legal advice.

**Law 18-07 of 10 June 2018**

On the protection of natural persons in the processing of personal data. Official Journal No. 34.

- [French text (PDF)](https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-francais/2018/F2018034.pdf)
- [Arabic text, the original (PDF)](https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-arabe/2018/A2018034.pdf)

**Law 25-11 of 24 July 2025**

Amends and completes Law 18-07. Official Journal No. 48.

- [French text (PDF)](https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-francais/2025/F2025048.pdf)
- [Arabic text, the original (PDF)](https://www.joradp.dz/FTP/jo-arabe/2025/A2025048.pdf)

## Questions

### Are my backups kept in Algeria too?

Yes. Database backups are kept in Algeria, in our two datacenters and on one more machine of ours in Algeria.

What happens to a database if one of our datacenters is lost is in [If one of our datacenters is lost](/databases/recovery#datacenter-loss).

### Does my traffic go through a service outside Algeria?

No. The addresses of your apps and databases point straight at our datacenters in Algeria. Our DNS provider only answers address lookups and never carries your traffic. If you put your own domain behind a proxy service, your traffic goes where that service sends it.

### Where is my source code?

Where you keep it, for example on GitHub. When you deploy, we fetch a copy, build it and run it in Algeria.

### Can I get a data processing agreement?

Yes, on request. Write to us from the contact page.
