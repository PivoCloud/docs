## Your AI agent

Every entry below links to the page that explains it in full.

- Let your agent restart, stop or start an app you shared with it, once you switch on the `Operate` level for that connection. Every action shows in the app's history with the agent's name: [what can an agent with Operate do](/agents/operate).
- Let your agent set, change or remove env vars on an app without ever seeing a value. The app takes the change the same safe way as a save from the console: [what can an agent with Operate do](/agents/operate).
- Undo a change to your env vars from the console. The app keeps its recent versions, and each entry says whether you or an agent made it: [how do I set environment variables for my app](/apps/environment-variables).
- Let your agent prepare an order for a new database, once you switch on the `Spend` level. It gets the price in DA and a link, and nothing is charged until you approve the order in the console with your password. You get an email for every order: [how does my AI agent order a database](/agents/orders).
- Stay in control when an agent acts too fast. A connection can make 10 changes in one hour. The next one is not done, the connection is frozen and you get an email. You lift the freeze from the console: [how do I see, change and cut off my AI agents](/agents/manage).
