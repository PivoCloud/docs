## Before you sign up

Every entry after the first links to the page that explains it in full.

- Read pivocloud.com in French. The home page, the prices, the contact page and the pages for managed PostgreSQL, app hosting and data residency have a French version, and a language switch in the top bar and the footer takes you to the other one. The console and these docs are in English for now.
- Read what each app plan and database tier includes, what it is limited to and what happens when you reach a limit, on the prices page and in the docs. An app or a database bought before 31 December 2026 keeps its price and resources as long as its subscription stays active: [what do my plan's limits mean](/plans-and-limits).
- Find out where your data is held: what stays in Algeria, which services are outside it and what each one receives, with links to the official text of Law 18-07: [where your data lives](/where-your-data-lives).
- See the date and the result of our latest weekly restore test on the home page, read on every visit. When the latest result is too old or cannot be read, the page shows no line at all: [the weekly restore drill](/databases/recovery#weekly-restore-drill).
- Get straight answers before you start: running in production, how your data is protected, prices and invoices, trying before you pay, papers for your company, and how to leave with your data: [common questions before you start](/common-questions).
- Call or send a WhatsApp message to our support number from any page of the site: [getting help](/getting-help).
