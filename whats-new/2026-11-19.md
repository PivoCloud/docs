## Your apps

Every entry below links to the page that explains it in full.

- Read your app's logs from the last 7 days, on every plan, even while the app is stopped or crashed. Lines from before a restart, a crash or a redeploy stay in one timeline, and you can pick a time range and search for a piece of text: [how long are my app's logs kept](/apps/logs).
- See when your app keeps crashing. The app page shows "Keeps crashing" instead of a healthy status, and your AI agent sees the same: [the app keeps crashing](/apps/troubleshooting#crash_loop).
- See when your app was stopped because it ran out of memory. The app page and the log history say so in one sentence that names your plan's memory: [the app ran out of memory](/apps/troubleshooting#out_of_memory).
- See your app's memory and processor use over the last hour, 24 hours or 7 days in the Usage tab, against your plan's limit in real figures, with the moments the app was slowed down, stopped, redeployed or changed plan: [how much is my app using](/apps/usage).

## Your databases

- See your database's memory, processor, storage and connections over the last hour, 24 hours or 7 days in the Usage tab, against your tier, with storage shown against your included storage: [how much is my database using](/databases/usage).
