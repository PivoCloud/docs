## What you can see while you wait

Some things on PivoCloud are decided by a person, not by the platform. This page
lists what you can do today while one of those decisions is pending. Every entry in this section
links to the page that explains it in full.

### October 2026

- See on your dashboard that your starting credit claim is being reviewed. One line stays there until the claim is decided, and the `Free Credits` card on the `Wallet` page shows the same claim as `Pending Review`: [what does it cost, and when am I charged](/billing/credit-and-charges).
- Ask for a custom domain invitation from your app's `Domains` tab when your plan does not include custom domains. The `Request an invitation` button sends the request with the app and its plan already attached, and an invitation covers every app on your account: [how do I put my own domain on my app](/apps/custom-domains).

New entries appear here when there is something new for you to use. There is no
schedule, so nothing on this page is waiting for a date.

## Your dashboard sign-in

- No app hosted on PivoCloud can sign you in as someone else or replace your dashboard sign-in.
