## Your apps and databases

- Your apps and databases keep running while PivoCloud updates itself. An update no longer rebuilds the platform in place, so the console and the API are back after one short restart instead of several.
- Before an update is declared done, PivoCloud checks that every app and database that answered before the update still answers after it. If one stops answering, PivoCloud puts the previous version of the platform back on its own, in seconds, and your app or database answers again without any action from you.
- If an update interrupts a deploy you started, the deploy starts again on its own: [a platform update interrupted your deploy](/apps/troubleshooting#deploy_interrupted_by_platform).
