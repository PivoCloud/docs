## Your apps

Every entry below links to the page that explains it in full.

- Roll your app back to the version it ran before the current one, from the deployment history, in one click and without a rebuild: [how do I roll my app back to the previous version](/apps/rollback).
- See which commit each deployment runs. Each deploy row in the history shows the short commit, its message and its branch, and the short commit opens on GitHub: [how do I deploy my first app](/apps/deploy).
- Deploy a repository that contains symlinks. PivoCloud builds it the way `docker build` does on your own computer: [can my repository contain symlinks](/apps/symlinks).
- Read a failed deploy as one plain sentence that says what happened and what to do next: [why did my deploy fail](/apps/troubleshooting).
- Get a clear answer when PivoCloud has no room left for a new app. The app is not created and nothing is charged: [how do I deploy my first app](/apps/deploy).
